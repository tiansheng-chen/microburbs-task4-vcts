What these measures mean

MedAE is the median absolute dollar error: half the predictions are closer than that amount. Within A$100 is the share at or below that error. R² measures explained variation in weekly asking rent.

The exact local scoring cohort

There are 4,083 adverts in the May–July window. We score the 3,956 with known advert parking and exclude 127 with unknown parking. 3,029 are within A$100; 927 are outside it.

This local rule differs from the organiser’s target-ID filter and parking fallback. These are not official hidden-test scores.

Which model does the A$47.58 score describe?

It describes the April-trained local-check model. The submitted CSV comes from a separate July refit using the same settings and feature list. That refit includes adverts from the earlier local-check period, so the reported check is not an independent evaluation of the submitted fitted model.

No August–September judging labels were used. The report discloses the refit rather than assigning the April-model score to it.

Every training row receives history strictly earlier than its own advert date. Each validation window uses one fixed forecast origin. The saved selection record specifies use_history=true and use_transit=false; the finalization script validates that record before scoring. The record documents the workflow, rather than an independently timestamped audit of every development decision.

04 · Method

One fitted model, two parking scenarios

1 · Flat attributesBedrooms, bathrooms, parking, latitude, longitude, suburb, property type and market time.
2 · Earlier rent historyPrevious asking rent and its age; a 365-day building median and advert count for the same bedroom group.
3 · Gradient boostingscikit-learn HistGradientBoostingRegressor with absolute-error loss and native missing-value handling.
4 · Two predictionsSet the parking indicator to 1, then 0, retaining all other inputs for that flat.

Building comparables share suburb, building address and bedroom count. Their median requires at least three earlier adverts in the preceding 365 days. Same-day and future adverts are excluded. The label-training window covers ten months; the history lookup may use older records already available at that origin.

  • 63 maximum leaves
  • 30 minimum samples per leaf
  • Learning rate 0.04
  • 400 iterations
  • L2 penalty 1
  • Random seed 42
  • Early stopping disabled

The model minimises absolute-error loss, which encourages accurate typical rents; training loss and the official median-error ranking are related but different objectives. Transit distances are not inputs to this final pipeline.

06 · Limits

What this result does not establish

  • Local evaluation is a proxy. The organiser evaluates later adverts with its own target membership and parking fallback. Our known-parking cohort is not identical.
  • Parking effects are model scenarios. For 1,618 flats (2.46%), the predicted rent with parking is lower. We preserve those predictions and disclose the limitation; the model has no monotonic parking constraint.
  • Past asking rents do not describe every change. Renovation, view, aspect, condition and a changed listing strategy can weaken the historical signal. The output estimates asking rent, not the amount eventually agreed.
  • Development comparisons are not controlled feature ablations. The selected history-aware pipeline differs from the grouped baseline and basic HGB in more than one respect. Its improvement is not an isolated causal contribution from history.
  • Transport provenance is separate. Earlier teammate-supplied transit-v2 features and a later documented GTFS processing workflow have different evidence chains. Neither transport comparison nor its July snapshot is used to justify the final no-transit score in this report.
  • The July refit has no separate local test. Its settings are unchanged, but it uses labels from the April-model evaluation period. The A$47.58 figure must not be presented as an independently measured error of that final fit.
